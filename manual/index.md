# Magic Panel

Firmware and documentation for the IA-PARTS **Magic Panel**: an 8×8 LED panel driven by an
ATmega328P and two MAX7221 drivers, controlled by its own rotary switch and jumpers or by a
droid controller over I2C.

- [Flash the firmware](install/flashing.md)
- [Wire it up](connect/wiring.md)
- [Run it standalone](use/standalone.md) or [drive it over I2C](use/i2c-quickstart.md)
- [I2C protocol reference](reference/i2c-protocol.md)
